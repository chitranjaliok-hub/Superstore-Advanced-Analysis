# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis — Tableau

An interactive Tableau business intelligence project analyzing sales, profitability, discount impact, regional performance, customer segmentation, and pricing scenarios using the Sample Superstore dataset.

## Project Overview

This project transforms the Superstore dataset into an interactive business intelligence solution designed to help understand:

- Overall sales and profitability
- Product and sub-category performance
- The relationship between discounts and profit
- Sales growth and regional performance
- Customer value and behavior
- Potential profitability under different discount scenarios

The analysis is presented through five interactive Tableau dashboards organized into a Tableau Story Book.


## Business Objectives

The project aims to answer key business questions:

- How are sales and profit performing overall?
- Which categories and sub-categories contribute most to profitability?
- How does discounting affect profit?
- How does performance vary across regions and states?
- Which customers represent the highest-value segments?
- How are sales changing over time?
- What could happen to profitability under different discount scenarios?
- What actions should management prioritize?

## Dashboards

### 1. Sales & Profit Executive Dashboard

Provides an executive-level overview of business performance through:

- Total Sales KPI
- Total Profit KPI
- Profit Margin KPI
- Metric relationship analysis
- Dynamic metric selection

### 2. Profitability & Discount Analysis

Analyzes profitability at product and customer levels using:

- Sub-category performance
- Discount vs. profit analysis
- Customer profitability analysis
- Sub-category Pareto analysis

### 3. Sales Growth & Regional Analysis

Examines sales trends and geographical performance through:

- Quarterly sales growth
- Regional sales performance
- State-level sales and profit map
- Sales and profitability comparisons across regions

### 4. Discount Scenario Simulator

An interactive what-if analysis that allows users to change the discount scenario and evaluate its potential effect on profitability.

The scenario analysis indicates that around a 20% discount, scenario profit is close to the actual profit, while higher discount levels substantially reduce profitability.

Note: The scenario simulator is an assumption-based model intended to explore potential profitability outcomes under alternative discount levels.

### 5. Customer RFM Analysis

Uses Recency, Frequency, and Monetary Value (RFM) to segment customers based on purchasing behavior.

The analysis identifies five customer segments:

- Champions
- Loyal Customers
- Potential Loyalists
- At Risk
- Lost Customers

The dashboard also allows users to interact with customer segments and examine their profitability and underlying RFM profiles.

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Protect profitability from excessive discounting

Discounting should be used selectively and its impact on profit should be monitored rather than focusing only on sales growth.

### 2. Prioritize high-value customers

Champions and Loyal Customers represent valuable customer groups that should receive strong retention and relationship-building strategies.

### 3. Develop Potential Loyalists

Customers showing promising purchasing behavior can be targeted with personalized offers designed to increase frequency and long-term value.

### 4. Address At Risk and Lost Customers selectively

Re-engagement strategies can be considered where the potential customer value justifies the effort.

### 5. Investigate regional and sub-category differences

Regional and profitability analyses can help identify where sales growth translates into healthy profit and where it does not.

### 6. Use scenario analysis for pricing decisions

The discount simulator can support management decisions by showing potential profitability consequences before changing discount policies.
